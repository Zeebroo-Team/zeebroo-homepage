Opening POS Settings

From the POS ribbon tab → Configure group → click POS Settings. The settings panel has three tabs: General, Accounts, and Receipt.

General Settings

SettingWhat it controls
Display ThemeLight, Dark, or follow the OS setting. Dark mode reduces eye strain in dim environments.
Featured Products LimitHow many products appear in the POS grid before pagination kicks in.
Featured Categories LimitNumber of categories shown in the quick-filter bar at the top of the POS grid.
Discount FieldShow or hide the discount field in the checkout cart.
Checkout ModalShow a confirmation modal before finalising a sale.
Show Service ProductsInclude service-bound products in the POS grid for service businesses.

Accounts Settings

Configure which bank account receives payments by default:

  • Default Deposit Account — the account where all POS cash receipts are credited
  • Payment Settlement Mode — Immediate settles each sale to the account right away; End of Day batches all sales and settles during the EOD process

Receipt Customisation

Control what appears on printed and digital receipts:

SettingEffect
Header TextCustom text printed above the itemised list (e.g. "Thank you for shopping with us!")
Footer TextCustom text printed at the bottom (e.g. exchange policy, website URL)
Show Business NamePrint the business name at the top of the receipt
Show Business AddressPrint the address below the business name
Show Account InfoPrint bank account details for customers who pay by transfer

Toggling Dark Mode

You can also toggle dark mode quickly from the VIEW ribbon tab → Appearance group → Dark Mode. No restart required — the interface switches immediately.

Checking for Updates

Click your profile name (top-right) → Check for Updates. The app contacts the Zeebroo server and compares the installed version against the latest release. If an update is available, a download link is shown for your platform (Windows, macOS, or Linux).

Signing Out

Click your profile name (top-right) → Sign Out, or click Sign Out in the VIEW ribbon's Account group. Your local session clears and the login screen reappears. Your data on the server is never affected by signing out.

Important If you switch branches or businesses, any unsaved POS cart data will be lost. Ensure all active sales are completed or parked before switching.
Real-time Sync Zeebroo POS automatically checks for changes to products, stock, tables, and menu items every 30 seconds. When another device or user updates data, your screen refreshes silently — no manual refresh needed.
